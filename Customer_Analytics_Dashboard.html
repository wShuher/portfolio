Customer Analytics Dashboard

Interactive business intelligence and churn forecasting platform equipped with dual-line sales metrics, scoring gauges, and cohort segmentation.

Click image to open high-resolution lightbox
Architecture: Full Stack Analytics Domain: Churn Prediction & CRM Role: Software Developer
C# / WinForms Django React Python Data Visualization SQL

System Overview

Customer information is centralized into a responsive visual workspace detailing critical attributes including customer IDs, demographic profiles, acquisition date, and chronological purchase histories.

Visits and sales volume are tracked concurrently through real-time dual-line charts with hover-state tooltips revealing precise monetary values and transaction timestamps. Recent purchases are structured in an interactive tabular grid displaying SKU IDs, descriptions, unit costs, quantity breakdown, and cumulative sales totals.

Customer loyalty and retention are modeled through a dynamic scoring system rendered via circular progress gauges. Predictive line graphs forecast churn likelihood across distinct customer cohorts (new, VIP, and standard), empowering marketing teams to initiate proactive retention workflows.
