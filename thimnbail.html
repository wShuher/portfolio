Thimbles 3D

Fast-paced 3D shell game and visual perception simulator with dynamic cup shuffling velocities and uncompromised fair RNG tracking.

Click image to open high-resolution lightbox
Platform: Android / Mobile Role: Solo Game Developer Engine: Unity3D
Unity3D C# Kinematic Animations Fair RNG Model Haptic Feedback

Gameplay & Mechanics

A faithful, high-speed 3D realization of the age-old shell game: three thimbles and one hidden ball. Watch intently as the wooden caps slide, orbit, and swap across the mahogany table at accelerating speeds before testing your tracking accuracy.

Unlike traditional street variations, the simulation guarantees 100% fair ball physics with no sleight-of-hand gimmicks. Built with kinematic interpolation curves, smooth camera tracking, and progressive speed scaling.
