System Overview

AiQONassist is an enterprise AI chatbot SaaS that automatically converts corporate websites and internal repositories into an intelligent, 24/7 knowledge-driven assistant. Powered by OpenAI models, semantic vector embeddings, and an asynchronous crawling engine.

Businesses can scan their live URLs, auto-discover complex sitemaps, index site content into a structured knowledge base, schedule automated recrawls, and fully customize the AI's persona, colors, and mascot to match their brand.

All Projects Inquire About This Project

Core Platform Capabilities

Automated Crawler & Sitemap

Auto-discovers website hierarchies by traversing internal links. Supports selective page indexing, manual URL insertions, and automated recurring crawls (monthly/weekly) to ensure zero data drift.

Persona & White-Label Theming

Complete visual and behavioral customization: adjust bot persona tones (e.g., Professional, Friendly), tailored welcome greetings, brand and bubble color pickers, and custom mascot URLs (image or Lottie animations).

Context-Aware AI Assistant

Lightweight embeddable web widget delivering instant, grounded answers about company services, pricing tiers, and product documentation with real-time streaming dialogue and minimal token latency.
